Research Personal

Andrin Bertschi

I am a doctoral student in the Secure & Trustworthy Systems Group at ETH Zurich. I build open-source confidential computing systems that address practical security challenges. My current work focuses on the Arm Confidential Computing Architecture (Arm CCA), including platform enablement, trusted I/O, and confidential mobile computing.

Before starting my PhD, I worked as a research engineer at ETH Zurich and as a software engineer at AXA. I hold an M.Sc. and B.Sc. in Computer Science from ETH Zurich and a Swiss Federal VET Diploma in Software Engineering.
